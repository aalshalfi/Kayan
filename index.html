About Kayan Trading Inc.

Strong trading bridges between Canada and global markets.

Founded in 2025 in Ottawa, Kayan Trading Inc. builds strong trading bridges between Canada and global markets through import, export, wholesale, and retail operations.

Our mission is to deliver quality, innovation, and trust across every product we offer — starting with our flagship venture in premium perfumes tailored for the Canadian market.
